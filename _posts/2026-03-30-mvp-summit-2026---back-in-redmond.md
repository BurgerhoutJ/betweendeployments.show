</div></a><p>Finally, the jetlag has disappeared and after some preparations for the upcoming renovation at home, I have now found time to write a piece about the past MVP Summit.</p>
<p>Last week I attended the <strong>Microsoft MVP Summit 2026</strong> in Redmond, Seattle. An inspiring week full of technical discussions, reconnecting with the community, and sharing feedback directly with the Microsoft product teams.</p>
<p>In this post I'll share some highlights of the week. As always, I will not share anything covered by NDA, but I will focus on the experience, learnings, and moments that stood out.</p>
<h1>What is the MVP Summit?</h1>
<p>The Microsoft MVP Summit is an annual event where MVPs from around the world come together at the Microsoft campus in Redmond.</p>
<p>The event focuses on:</p>
<ul>
<li><p>direct interaction with Microsoft product teams</p></li>
<li><p>deep technical sessions</p></li>
<li><p>sharing feedback from the field</p></li>
<li><p>connecting with other MVPs</p></li>
<li><p>learning about upcoming developments</p></li>
</ul>
<p>Most sessions are under NDA, which allows for open discussions and honest feedback between the community and Microsoft.</p>
<p>For me personally, this event is always valuable because it connects real-world customer challenges with the people building the products.</p>
<h1>Travelling to Seattle (Saturday)</h1>
<p>Traveling always feels like it’s really about to begin. The downside is sitting in a plane for about 10 hours. For me, the journey went via Paris to Seattle.</p>
<p>At Schiphol I met <strong><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/oktaysari/?ref=burgerhout.org">Oktay</a>, <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/sanderberkouwer/?ref=burgerhout.org">Sander</a> and <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/raymondcomvalius/?ref=burgerhout.org">Raymond</a></strong>, so the four of us travelled to Seattle on the same flights.</p>
<p>Once we arrived at Seattle Airport, we had to wait for the rest of the Intune MVPs from the Netherlands, as they were on another flight. After that it was time to pick up the car and drive to Bellevue to check in.</p>
<p>Of course, the day ended with food and drinks at <strong>Joey’s</strong>.</p>

<p>On Sunday mornings we now traditionally go on a hike with a group of MVPs and Microsoft people, organized by <strong><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/sangeev/?ref=burgerhout.org">Sangee</a></strong>.</p>

<p>Afterwards we drove to an outlet, had lunch, did some shopping and then returned to the hotel, as we had to be in Seattle later that day for a party hosted by the <strong><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/company/workplace-ninjas-us/?ref=burgerhout.org">Workplace Ninja US</a></strong> team.</p>
