<p>Welcome to <strong>Between Deployments</strong>. </p>
<p>I’m Jeroen Burgerhout, and this is a show about modern workplace IT — Intune, Entra ID, Windows 365, Autopilot, and conditional access policies.</p>
<p>No fixed schedule here. I record when there’s someone in the field with something worth saying — a war story, a fix, a good rant about Conditional Access. If there’s nothing worth recording, you don’t hear from me. That’s the deal.</p>
<p>If you’ve ever waited on a sync or explained Entra ID to a confused exec for the fifth time — you’re in the right place.</p>
<p>I’m Jeroen Burgerhout. See you whenever there’s something worth talking about.</p>
